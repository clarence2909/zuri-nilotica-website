Why Uganda matters

Nilotica is not generic shea.

Nilotica shea grows across parts of East Africa, including Uganda's shea landscapes. It is valued for a naturally softer, creamier texture that melts easily into skin and hair.

That origin is part of the product. It explains the feel, the simplicity, and why Zuri Nilotica can stay focused on one ingredient instead of fragrance, fillers, or heavy processing.

From tree to butter

Protect the source

The shea tree matters.

Shea butter is only possible when shea trees remain standing. The story behind Zuri Nilotica is therefore also a story of source care: valuing the tree, the landscape, and the people who know how to work with it.

Our sourcing message is simple: beauty should not disconnect the customer from the place the ingredient comes from.
