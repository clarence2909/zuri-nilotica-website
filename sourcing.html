What to know

Why Nilotica feels different.

Nilotica shea grows in East Africa, including parts of Uganda. Compared with many heavier shea butters, it is loved for a softer, creamier feel that warms quickly in the palm and melts easily into skin and hair.

That is why Zuri Nilotica can stay simple. The texture already feels special, so the formula does not need added fragrance, filler, or unnecessary extras.

Before it reaches your bathroom shelf, the shea nuts are collected, sorted, dried, and prepared with care. That process helps protect the soft texture customers look for.

Why the tree matters

No shea tree, no shea butter.

Shea butter only exists because shea trees continue to grow and produce fruit. Protecting those trees protects future harvests and keeps the source alive.

Across Northern Uganda, shea trees can be threatened by charcoal burning, land pressure, and careless harvesting. We want customers to understand that a good shea product begins with respect for the tree.
