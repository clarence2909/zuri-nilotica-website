What to know

Why this shea feels different.

Vitellaria nilotica grows wild along the East African Nile belt. It naturally contains up to 57% oleic acid, compared with about 40% in standard shea, contributing to its lower melting point and silky feel.

The result is a smooth, non-grainy butter that melts on contact and absorbs quickly, with no synthetic fillers or fragrance.

Wild fallen nuts are gathered, sorted, dried, and cold-pressed with care through direct partnerships with women-led cooperatives.
