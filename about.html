About Us

Skin care in its purest state.

Zuri Nilotica was created around one hero ingredient: Vitellaria nilotica, the East African shea tree known for a softer, silkier butter. Our work is to carry that texture from Uganda's shea-growing landscapes to your skin without diluting its character.

We believe pure shea butter should feel modern, useful, and easy to trust. Every jar is built around clarity: one ingredient, careful sourcing, honest handling, and a ritual that fits into daily care.
