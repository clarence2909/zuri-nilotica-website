Zuri Nilotica — Master Marketing Plan
BRAND POSITIONING, PRESENTATION BLUEPRINT & GO-TO-MARKET EXECUTION
Brand Entity: Zuri Nilotica Document Type: Team Strategy & Execution Roadmap
Primary Channel: Amazon UK (FBA) / Direct Skincare Date: July 2026
1. Strategic Overview & Core Narrative
This marketing strategy moves Zuri Nilotica away from the overcrowded, low-margin "raw commodity" space
and positions it as a rare, high-performance skincare ritual.
The Core Strategic Pivot
Old Consumer Perception: "Shea butter is hard, greasy, and basic—you have to melt it in your hands."
The Zuri Nilotica Pivot: "Nilotica is a cold-pressed, silky, rare skin balm that absorbs instantly, ethically sourced
directly from women's cooperatives in Northern Uganda."
2. Compliance-First Messaging Framework
Due to cosmetic regulations in global markets (UK, EU, US), marketing must strictly focus on beautifying,
hydrating, and restoring the skin barrier rather than making drug/medical claims.
❌ NON-COMPLIANT (Medical / Drug Claims) ✅ COMPLIANT (Cosmetic & Structural Claims)
"Heals or cures eczema, psoriasis, acne" "Deeply soothes and restores dry, stressed skin"
"Reduces medical skin inflammation" "Rich in Oleic Acid to reinforce the skin moisture barrier"
"Repairs damaged cellular tissue" "Nourishes and softens rough, textured skin"
3. Presentation Strategy: "Unrefined Luxury"
The core presentation challenges common audience assumptions and presents a simple, effective solution.
SLIDE 1: THE HOOK
Visual: Close-up video of Zuri Nilotica’s silky butter melting on contact with skin.
Message: "What if the ultimate dry-skin solution isn't a complex 10-step chemical serum, but a rare single-
ingredient balm cold-pressed at the source?"
SLIDE 2: CHALLENGING THE AUDIENCE (THE MYTH)
Visual: Side-by-side comparison of hard West African shea vs. smooth Zuri Nilotica.
Message: "Most people think shea butter is hard, thick, and greasy. That’s because 95% of the market sells
Vitellaria Paradoxa from West Africa. It works, but it takes work."
SLIDE 3: THE REVEAL & FUNCTIONAL SCIENCE
Visual: Breakdown of naturally occurring Oleic Acid and Vitamin E content.
Message: "Meet Vitellaria Nilotica. Growing wild along the East African Nile belt, it contains up to 57% Oleic
Acid (vs ~40% in standard shea). The result: zero graininess, lower melting point, and instant absorption."

SLIDE 4: ETHICAL SOURCING & WOMEN'S COOPERATIVES
Visual: Authentic imagery of women harvesters in Northern Uganda collecting wild fallen nuts.
Message: "Direct partnerships with women-led cooperatives in Northern Uganda. Fair-trade premiums

SLIDE 5: THE OFFER (SIMPLICITY MEETS LUXURY)
Visual: Cosmetic-grade dark glass jar of Zuri Nilotica (100g trial and 250g core sizes).

4. Step-by-Step Execution Plan for Marketing Team
Phase 1: Content & Asset Creation (Weeks 1–3)
• Action 1.1: Produce high-resolution texture videos demonstrating immediate melting on skin.
• Action 1.2: Draft Amazon A+ Content emphasizing cold-pressed extraction, East vs. West African shea
differences, and cooperative impact stats.
Phase 2: Channel Execution & Amazon Go-To-Market (Weeks 4–6)
• Action 2.1: Ensure 100% Prime-eligible FBA fulfillment.
• Action 2.2: Target long-tail PPC keywords: "Nilotica shea butter for dry skin", "Cold pressed organic facial
balm", "Non grainy natural eczema cream alternative".
• Action 2.3: Price core 250g jar at £18.99; position 100g travel jar at £12.99 as a low-friction trial option.
Phase 3: Influencer Seeding & Social Proof (Weeks 6–10)
• Action 3.1: Seed 50–100 micro-influencers (clean beauty, curly hair, dry skin advocates).
• Action 3.2: Request "First Reaction / Texture Comparison" video demonstrations.
• Action 3.3: Repurpose UGC into Amazon brand storefront assets and social ad creatives.
Phase 4: Retention & Customer Lifetime Value (Ongoing)
• Action 4.1: Include premium packaging insert cards sharing the Northern Uganda cooperative story.
• Action 4.2: Offer an upgrade discount code incentivizing 100g trial buyers to purchase the 250g core jar.
